How Binary File Size Fractions Work

🖼️ 1. Bitmap Images (Numerator)

Every pixel stores a binary colour code. Total bits = total pixels × bits per pixel:

Width × Height × Colour Depth

Watch out: If given number of colours (e.g. 16 colours), find the power of 2: 2⁴ = 16 → 4 bits.

🔊 2. Digital Sound (Numerator)

Sound waves are sampled every second. Multiply samples per second by bits per sample and total seconds:

Sample Rate × Resolution × Seconds × Channels

Watch out: Convert minutes to seconds (× 60). Mono = 1 channel, Stereo = 2 channels.

🪜 3. Binary Units (Denominator)

The numerator gives Bits. Divide on the bottom line to scale up using binary IEC units (1024):

  • • To Bytes: ÷ 8
  • • To KiB: ÷ 8 × 1024
  • • To MiB: ÷ 8 × 1024 × 1024
